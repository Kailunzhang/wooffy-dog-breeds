    How these numbers work

    Each breed’s figures come from its Wooffy first-year cost guide. The calculator adds up the line items for the options you pick: the puppy price or adoption fee, year-one vet care, spay/neuter, 12 months of food and insurance, grooming, and setup. Items a guide doesn’t list are filled in with the typical figure for dogs of the same size and marked “(estimate)”. The region setting moves vet, spay/neuter and grooming prices about 10% down or 15% up, a rough guide, not local pricing (for scale, BEA regional price parities put 2023 price levels outside metro areas about 12% below the US average, and in the priciest metro areas 13% to 18% above it).     Is adopting a dog cheaper than buying from a breeder?

    Usually. Shelter and rescue fees usually run $50–$500, with puppies at the high end, according to Petfinder. Adoption fees often cover first vaccines, spay/neuter and a microchip, so ask what’s included. The median breeder price range in our guides is __CALC_PUPPY_MED__.

    Why doesn’t the total match the breed’s cost guide?

    The calculator adds up every line item for the options you choose. A guide’s headline total may be rounded or leave out the puppy price, and items a guide doesn’t list are filled in with the typical figure for dogs of the same size.
